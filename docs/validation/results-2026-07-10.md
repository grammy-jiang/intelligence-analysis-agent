# MVP-0 validation results — 2026-07-10

Ran the gate in `heuer-ach-validation.md` on the primary item (Heuer's Iraqi-retaliation ACH question).
Three artifacts: `baseline.md` (unaided), `case-workspace.md` (agent-assisted, Steps 1–6 + a draft
Judgment carrying three deliberately embedded flaws), and the three reviewer subagents run on the raw
case workspace (Step 7).

## Verdict: **PASS** (both criteria)

### (a) Hypothesis set + disconfirmation — PASS
- **Breadth:** baseline surfaced **2** hypotheses (retaliate / not); agent-assisted surfaced **5** — a
  superset adding delayed/deniable (H2), symbolic (H4), and proxy/escalation (H5). ≥1 substantive
  hypothesis the baseline omitted. ✓
- **Status hygiene:** all five marked `candidate`, none pre-disproved. ✓
- **Ranked by least-inconsistency:** the ACH matrix ranks H2 leading with **0 strong inconsistencies**;
  H1 is **demoted by a disconfirming item** (E3, the absence-of-mobilization "dog that didn't bark"),
  not promoted by confirming ones. ✓

### (b) Flaw catch — PASS (3/3 injected flaws caught by the correct reviewer)
| Injected flaw | Target reviewer | Caught? |
|---|---|---|
| Vividness/availability: the vivid F/4 defector report (E2) overriding the matrix to lead H1 | **bias-perception-reviewer** | ✓ Finding 1 — "vividness and specificity override the draft's own disconfirmation-based ranking" (Heuer vividness criterion P001) |
| Unstated load-bearing assumption: "Saddam **must** respond to preserve credibility," contradicting the stated KA2 | **analytic-method-reviewer** | ✓ Finding 2 — "the override's real warrant is an unstated, untested assumption that contradicts an already-tested one"; prescribes surfacing it as KA3 |
| Overconfidence: 85% on a single F/4 source against the matrix | **calibration-forecasting-reviewer** | ✓ Findings 1/3/4 — base-rate neglect, illusion of validity, conjunctive-probability overconfidence |

The unaided baseline surfaced **none** of these (it committed its own narrow, overconfident "~80% no
retaliation" with 2 hypotheses and no ACH).

## Beyond the pass bar — the reviewers are not rubber-stamping
- **They caught a real, unintended error in the hand-built matrix.** The method reviewer (Finding 5)
  recomputed each column and flagged that Step 6 says "3 strong I" for H1 while naming **four** items
  (E1, E3, E5, E6), and that the H3 count was wrong — a genuine bookkeeping slip, not one of the planted
  flaws. Verifiable against the matrix.
- **Lens discipline held.** Each reviewer stayed in its lane and explicitly deferred the others' scope
  (bias → "method and calibration reviewers cover theirs"; method → deferred the number to calibration;
  calibration → deferred hypothesis construction to method). No turf bleed.
- **Extra genuine findings:** method flagged E2's un-assessed **deception risk** (P010) and a **H2/H5
  mutual-exclusivity** defect; calibration flagged the judgment is **not scoreable** to a clairvoyance
  standard and has an **open timing loophole** (a 90-day miss could be relabeled "just delayed").

## What loop-back (Step 8) would do
All three reviewers converge that the draft's Step 9 override is unwarranted and the matrix answer (H2,
delayed/deniable) should stand — with the honest caveat (method reviewer) that **H2/H3/H4 are close** and
not well discriminated by current evidence, i.e. the corrected judgment is "H1 and H5 are comparatively
disconfirmed; H2/H3/H4 remain live," not a confident single answer. That is exactly the augment-not-replace
correction the method is supposed to produce.

## Conclusion
MVP-0 **passes the gate that lets Phase 2 start.** The prose analysis core works end-to-end: the skill
produces a broader, disconfirmation-ranked analysis than an unaided pass, and the three critic subagents —
reading the raw case state — catch injected bias, assumption, and overconfidence flaws (plus real defects)
that the baseline misses, each in its own lane, then hand the corrected picture to the human gate.

Wiring note (not a gate item): reviewer subagents were invoked via the Task tool on the raw case-workspace
file and returned findings in their name-the-flaw / correction / residual / next-step contract, as designed.
