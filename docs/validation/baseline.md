# Baseline (unaided, single-pass) — Iraqi retaliation question

> Control run: one pass, "give your best analysis," no ACH matrix, no reviewers. As-of late June 1993,
> shortly after the US cruise-missile strike on the Iraqi Intelligence Service (IIS) HQ.

**Question:** Will Iraq retaliate for the US strike?

**Analysis:** Saddam has just seen his intelligence headquarters destroyed and knows the US will strike
again if provoked. He is a rational actor who understands cost and benefit. The strike sent a clear
deterrent message. A dramatic recent defector report of an IIS terror plot is alarming, but on balance
Saddam will not risk further US retaliation so soon after being hit.

**Hypotheses considered:**
- H1: Iraq retaliates soon (terrorism against US targets).
- H2: Iraq does not retaliate (deterred).

**Conclusion:** Iraq will **not** retaliate. Confidence: **high (~80%)**. The strike deterred Saddam.
