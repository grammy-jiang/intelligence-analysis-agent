# Case workspace — Iraqi retaliation question (agent-assisted)

> Produced by the `structured-analysis` skill. As-of late June 1993, shortly after the US cruise-missile
> strike on the Iraqi Intelligence Service (IIS) HQ in Baghdad (retaliation for the plot to assassinate
> former President Bush). This is a DRAFT awaiting reviewer critique and human approval.

## Question *(Step 1 — Frame)*

Primary: **Will Iraq retaliate for the 26 June 1993 US strike — and if so, in what form and on what
horizon?**
Sub-questions: (a) near-term (0–3 mo) vs delayed? (b) attributable vs deniable? (c) against US persons,
interests, or symbolic/rhetorical only? (d) direct or via proxy?
Decision it serves: US force-protection posture and whether to pre-position warnings to soft targets.
Outside view: base rate of state retaliation after a limited punitive strike is **moderate but skewed to
delayed/deniable** forms for a regime under sanctions (reasoned, no tracker at MVP).

## Hypotheses *(Step 2)* — all mutually exclusive; status

- **H1** — Near-term violent, attributable retaliation (terrorism vs US targets, 0–3 mo). `candidate`
- **H2** — Delayed, deniable retaliation (covert, 3–24 mo, plausible deniability). `candidate`
- **H3** — No retaliation; absorb the blow / deterred. `candidate`
- **H4** — Symbolic / rhetorical response only (propaganda, diplomacy). `candidate`
- **H5** — Escalation via proxy or regional action. `candidate`

## Evidence *(Step 3 — with reliability A–F / credibility 1–6, expected observables, absence)*

- **E1** — Saddam's historical pattern favours **delayed, deniable** reprisal over immediate open attack.
  *(B/2.)* If H2 true, expect low near-term signature; if H1 true, expect near-term mobilization.
- **E2** — A **single defector** gives a vivid, detailed account of an imminent IIS terror cell poised to
  strike a US embassy. *(F/4 — untested, uncorroborated, defector may have incentive.)* This is the most
  alarming report in the file and dominates current discussion.
- **E3** — **No unusual IIS operational activity** detected since the strike (comms, travel, safehouse
  signatures all nominal). *(B/2.)* Absence of the mobilization H1 would require — the dog that did not bark.
- **E4** — Iraqi state media issues threatening rhetoric promising a "response." *(A/3.)* Consistent with
  H4 and with cover for H2; weakly diagnostic.
- **E5** — The strike **degraded IIS external-operations capability** (the HQ node was hit). *(B/2.)* Raises
  the cost/time for H1; consistent with H2/H3.
- **E6** — Iraq is under heavy sanctions and seeks eventual relief; overt attribution would harden the US
  and allies. *(A/2.)* Consistent with H2/H3/H4; inconsistent with H1/H5.

## Key assumptions *(Step 5)*

- KA1 — The current absence of IIS activity (E3) reflects true intent, not superior operational security.
  *(confidence: medium; undermined if Iraq is running a compartmented cell below our collection.)*
- KA2 — Saddam weighs this decision on a cost–benefit calculus and will be deterred by expected US cost.
  *(confidence: stated as high.)*

## Ranking *(Step 6 — by LEAST inconsistency)*

Down each column, count strong inconsistencies:

- **H2 delayed/deniable — 0 strong I** → **leading** (hardest to disprove).
- H3 none — 1 (E4). H4 symbolic — 1 (E4 fits, but E1/E5 also fit). H1 near-term — 3 strong I (E1, E3, E5,
  E6). H5 proxy — 3 strong I.
- E2 (the defector plot) is the ONLY item pulling toward H1, and it is the weakest-graded item (F/4);
  E3's absence directly contradicts an imminent plot. H1 is demoted despite E2's vividness.

## Judgment *(Step 9 — draft)*

Leading hypothesis: **H1, near-term violent retaliation.** Although the matrix (Step 6) shows H2 with the
fewest inconsistencies, the specificity and severity of the defector's account (E2) make an imminent attack
the dominant concern — a threat this concrete and alarming cannot be discounted. Saddam has just been
humiliated and **must** respond in kind to preserve his deterrent credibility. We therefore assess **with
high confidence (85%) that Iraq will mount a near-term (0–3 mo) attack on a US target.** Residual ~15%.
